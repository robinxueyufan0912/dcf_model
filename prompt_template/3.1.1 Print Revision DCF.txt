- pre-print = 最新最近的财报前
- post-print = 最新最近的财报后
- short term = reported Q / next Q / current FY
- long term = FY+1 to FY+3
- terminal = FY+4 以后

Revenue, GM, NI, FCF, PV, delta, delta %, revision direction, confidence, source。

然后给出 PV bridge：
short-term PV delta, long-term PV delta, terminal PV delta, net debt/share count delta, total equity value delta, per-share delta。
